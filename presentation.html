03 / CORE INNOVATION #1

AI Vision Pathology & Regional Voice Read-Aloud

5-Step Computer Vision Pipeline

  • 1. Camera Framing: Custom HTML5 viewfinder with leaf bounding box and torch.
  • 2. Blur & Exposure Filter: Laplacian variance rejects poor photos (< 35 variance).
  • 3. Neural Diagnosis: Detects Tomato Early Blight, Chilli Leaf Curl, Powdery Mildew with 90%+ match.
  • 4. Severity Meter: Classifies invasion into Healthy, Mild, Moderate, or Severe.
  • 5. Regional Audio TTS: Web Speech API reads prescriptions aloud in Telugu / Hindi / English.
Live Diagnostic Example

Tomato — Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

94% Confidence AI Match | Severe Invasion

Curative Action: Prune bottom yellowing leaves. Apply Trichoderma bio-fungicide @ 5g/L or Neem oil 3000 PPM. Switch from overhead sprinkler to drip irrigation.

05 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Cold Storage Network & Rural Farm Transport

🗺️Interactive Leaflet Map

Real-world geo-coordinates mapping accredited cold storage facilities across Guntur, Tenali, and Krishna districts with calculated distance from the farmer's village.

📊Live Metric Ton (MT) Capacity

Real-time warehouse capacity indicators (e.g. 500 MT available / 2,500 MT capacity) with temperature ranges suited for Chilli, Tomato, and Spices.

💳20% Advance Booking Fee

Farmers lock in storage bays ahead of peak harvest price crashes by depositing an upfront 20% advance via UPI or direct bank challan.

🚚Farm-Gate Transport Logistics

Integrated booking of Bolero (2 Ton) and Eicher (5 Ton) transport vehicles picking up produce directly from farms to storage depots.
